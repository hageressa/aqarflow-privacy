AqarFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

AqarFlow is a non-commercial demonstration project built for educational and development purposes. It is not offered as a product or service to the public, and it is not used to process data belonging to real customers.

Who we are

AqarFlow is a personal portfolio project demonstrating automated lead qualification across messaging channels. The project is operated by an individual developer and has no commercial operations.

Information we process

When a test user sends a message to our test number or test page, the following is processed:

  • The text content of the message
  • The sender identifier provided by the messaging platform
  • The name shown on the sender's public messaging profile, if available
  • The channel the message arrived from, and the time it was received

We do not collect payment details, government identifiers, precise location, health information, or any other sensitive category of personal data.

Why we process it

The sole purpose is to demonstrate an automated reply and lead-qualification workflow. Message content is passed to a language model to generate a reply and to extract stated preferences, such as a budget range or a preferred area. No automated decision produces a legal or similarly significant effect on any person.

Where it is stored

Processed data is stored in Google Sheets and HubSpot. Access is limited to the project owner. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is handled by the following service providers strictly to operate the demonstration: Meta Platforms (message delivery), Google (workflow automation, spreadsheet storage, and the Gemini language model), and HubSpot (contact records).

Retention

Test data is retained only for the duration of the demonstration and is deleted when the project period ends.

Your rights

Anyone who has interacted with the test number may request a copy of their data or ask for it to be deleted. Requests are actioned promptly at the contact address below.

Children

This project is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
